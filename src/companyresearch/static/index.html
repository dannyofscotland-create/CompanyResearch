What shorts pick on

Shorting (borrow shares, sell, hope the price falls) is usually legal. Spreading lies to crash a share is not. These names have the shape critics hunt — losses, cash burn, crowded shorts. Not a short list. This app cannot short. A squeeze can wreck the shorts too.
